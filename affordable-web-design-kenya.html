Frequently Asked Questions

Do you host the website after building it?

Yes. We can arrange reliable Kenya-based hosting for you, or deploy to your existing hosting. Domain and hosting fees are separate from the design fee and start at around KES 5,000–12,000/year.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every package includes a free support period. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance packages starting at KES 3,000/month covering updates, security and minor edits.

Do you require 100% payment upfront?

No. We work on a 50% deposit to start, 50% on final delivery and your approval. This protects both of us.

Can you add M-Pesa payment to my site?

Yes. M-Pesa integration (Daraja API or third-party gateways like Pesapal) is available as an add-on. See our e-commerce page for more details.
