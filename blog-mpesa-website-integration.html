M-Pesa Website Integration Guide for Kenyan Businesses (2025)

📅 October 2025 ✍️ Keniche Digital ⏱️ 8 min read

Over 30 million Kenyans use M-Pesa. If your website can't accept M-Pesa payments, you're leaving serious money on the table. Here's everything you need to know — in plain English.

Why M-Pesa Integration Matters

Cash is still king in many parts of Kenya, but mobile money is overtaking it fast. For any e-commerce store, booking site, or services business, accepting M-Pesa online means:

  • Customers pay instantly without leaving your website
  • You receive automatic payment confirmations
  • You can serve customers 24/7, even when you're asleep
  • You build trust — Kenyans trust M-Pesa completely

How M-Pesa Website Integration Works

Safaricom provides a developer platform called the Daraja API (Swahili for "Bridge"). It lets your website communicate directly with M-Pesa. Here's the customer experience when it's set up properly:

Step 1

Customer clicks "Pay with M-Pesa"

They enter their phone number (07XX XXX XXX) and confirm the amount.

Step 2

STK Push is sent to their phone

A pop-up appears on their Safaricom handset asking them to enter their M-Pesa PIN. This is called an "STK Push" (SIM Toolkit Push).

Step 3

They enter their PIN and confirm

Payment is processed in seconds. Both the customer and you receive an SMS confirmation.

Step 4

Your website updates automatically

The order is marked as paid, a receipt is sent, and inventory is updated — all automatically.

What You Need to Get Started

  1. An M-Pesa Business Account (Paybill or Till Number) — Apply at any Safaricom shop with your business registration documents (Certificate of Incorporation or Business Registration + KRA PIN)
  2. A Daraja API account — Register at developer.safaricom.co.ke (free)
  3. A live website with HTTPS — The Daraja API requires a secure (https://) website
  4. A developer to integrate it — This is where Keniche comes in
💡 Pro tip: If you're just starting out and don't have a Paybill yet, you can use third-party payment gateways like Pesapal, iPay Africa, or Flutterwave which support M-Pesa and are faster to set up — though they charge slightly higher transaction fees.

How Much Does M-Pesa Integration Cost?

The cost of integrating M-Pesa on your website in Kenya depends on the method and the developer:

  • Third-party gateway (Pesapal/iPay): KES 8,000–20,000 developer fee + gateway subscription
  • Direct Daraja API: KES 20,000–50,000 developer fee (more complex)
  • M-Pesa Paybill setup: Free (Safaricom) + monthly fee (varies)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Using sandbox credentials in production — test thoroughly in the sandbox environment first
  • ❌ Not handling payment callbacks — your site must listen for Safaricom's confirmation message
  • ❌ No timeout handling — what happens if a customer's phone is off? Your code must handle this gracefully
  • ❌ Storing M-Pesa credentials in plain text — always use environment variables
  • ❌ Not testing with real money — always run a live test transaction before going public

How Long Does Integration Take?

Realistically, here's what to expect:

  • Getting your Paybill: 3–10 business days (Safaricom approval)
  • Developer integration: 3–7 days for straightforward sites
  • Testing: 2–3 days
  • Total: 2–4 weeks from start to live
