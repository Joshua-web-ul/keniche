How Much Does a Website Cost in Kenya (2025)?

📅 October 2025 ✍️ Keniche Digital ⏱️ 6 min read

If you've been Googling "website design prices Kenya" and getting vague answers, this guide is for you. We break down real costs in KES — no sugarcoating, no hidden fees.

The Short Answer

A professionally designed website in Kenya costs anywhere from KES 15,000 to KES 300,000+ depending on complexity, the number of pages, and what features you need. Here's a proper breakdown:

Type Pages Price Range (KES) Timeline
Simple Landing Page 1 15,000 – 30,000 2–3 days
Business / SME Website 4–6 30,000 – 80,000 5–10 days
E-Commerce Store 10–30+ 60,000 – 150,000 10–21 days
Corporate / Enterprise 20+ 120,000 – 300,000+ 3–8 weeks
Custom Web System / Portal Custom 200,000+ Quoted

What's Included in the Price?

A good web designer in Nairobi will typically include:

  • Design & layout — custom, mobile-responsive design (not a free template)
  • Domain setup — your .co.ke or .com address
  • Web hosting — where your site lives online (usually first year included)
  • Contact form — so clients can reach you directly
  • WhatsApp button — critical for the Kenyan market
  • Basic SEO — so Google can find your site
  • SSL certificate — the "padlock" that makes your site secure (https://)

What Costs Extra?

Watch out for these add-ons that some agencies charge separately:

  • M-Pesa integration — KES 10,000–30,000 extra
  • Logo design — KES 5,000–15,000 if not included
  • Monthly maintenance — KES 3,000–8,000/month
  • Google Ads setup — KES 5,000–15,000 setup fee + ad spend
  • Stock photos / professional photos — KES 3,000–20,000

Why Are Some Websites KES 5,000?

You'll sometimes see websites advertised for KES 3,000–5,000 on social media. These are usually built on free website builders (like Wix or free WordPress themes) with no custom design, poor mobile performance, no SEO, and minimal support. They look unprofessional and often hurt rather than help your business.

A website is your 24/7 salesperson. Cutting corners here costs you more in the long run.

Ongoing Costs to Budget For

  • Domain renewal: KES 1,500–3,000/year
  • Web hosting: KES 5,000–15,000/year
  • Maintenance & updates: KES 3,000–8,000/month (optional but recommended)

How to Get the Best Value

  1. Get at least 2–3 quotes from different designers
  2. Ask to see their portfolio — real, live websites
  3. Ask about post-launch support — what happens if something breaks?
  4. Prioritize mobile — over 80% of Kenyans browse on phones
  5. Don't pay 100% upfront — typical is 50% to start, 50% on delivery
